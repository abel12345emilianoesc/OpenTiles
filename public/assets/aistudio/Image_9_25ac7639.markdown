## Brand & Style
The design system targets mobile rhythm gamers seeking an elevated, hypnotic audiovisual experience. It shifts away from chaotic, toy-like casual arcade conventions into a refined, high-end digital concert atmosphere: sleek, razor-sharp, and reactive.

The aesthetic synthesizes **Modern Dark Minimalism** with **Hyper-Refined Luminescence (Glassmorphism + Neon Bloom)**:
- **Atmosphere**: Deep, infinite space where music manifests as luminous glass prisms and electric sound waves.
- **Emotion**: Precision, flow-state immersion, rush of accomplishment, and sophisticated luxury.
- **Visual Weight**: Near-weightless floating glass surfaces anchored by intensely vibrant spectral lighting pulses triggered by acoustic precision (Perfect, Great, Miss).

## Colors
The palette relies on a cavernous canvas punctuated by electric spectral accents:

- **Canvas & Backdrops**: `#0B0F19` serves as the primary void, with `#070A11` used for deep ambient recesses and `#161F33` for elevated panel foundations.
- **Primary Violet (`#8B5CF6`)**: Represents melody, standard active rhythm lanes, and interactive progress states.
- **Secondary Cyan (`#06B6D4`)**: Represents resonance, long sustain tracks, precision hit waves, and tempo trackers.
- **Tertiary Magenta (`#F43F5E`)**: Reserved for critical events: peak combo multipliers, fever mode, streaks, and warning thresholds.
- **Chromatic Bleed & Glows**: Ambient gradients utilize 15% to 35% opacity radial stops blending Primary into Secondary or Tertiary, avoiding opaque color fills in favor of optical luminosity.

## Typography
Typography is tuned for instant readability under ultra-fast peripheral movement.

- **Primary Display & Headings (`Outfit`)**: Clean, geometric letterforms with open counters ensure digit recognition in fractions of a second. Used for dynamic score tracking, rank reveals, and high-impact messaging.
- **Technical & Metric Indicators (`Space Grotesk`)**: Monoline, technological letterforms delivering rigid, high-contrast feedback for combo labels, multipliers (`x4`, `x8`), BPM counters, and accuracy telemetry. All metrics employ tabular figures (`tnum`) to eliminate layout jitter during high-speed scoring.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres to a fixed 4-lane mobile viewport architecture during gameplay, transitioning to an edge-to-edge fluid card grid in lobby and song-selection states.

- **Gameplay Lane Architecture**: Exactly 4 vertical track columns divided by razor-thin `0.5rem` gutters. Interactive hit lines rest exactly `space-xl` above the device safe area to prevent tactile thumb obstruction.
- **HUD & Score Overlays**: Head-up displays hug top margins with symmetrical `margin` values, reserving visual vertical space for approaching note descent.
- **Rhythm Rhythm Grids**: Spacing strictly scales on multiples of 4px/8px to match musical meter and quantized touch feedback.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is created through frosted translucent glass substrates layered over pitch-black space, illuminated by luminous optical energy:

1. **Backdrop Void**: `#0B0F19` with ambient radial color bleeds (blurred at 80px-120px) indicating the current song's genre or mood.
2. **Track Lanes**: Transparent panels featuring subtle vertical boundary lines (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`).
3. **Glassmorphic Interactive Surfaces**: Tiles and interactive modules utilize `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` coupled with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a directional 1px perimeter highlight (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` on top, fading to transparent at the base).
4. **Active Luminescence & Hits**: Successful notes do not cast traditional black shadows; they project multi-layered neon glows:
   - Primary: `0 0 15px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.6)`
   - Bloom: `0 0 35px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.35)`
   - Critical Streak/Fever: `0 0 45px rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.8)`

## Shapes
A roundedness tier of 2 provides sleek modern corners without softening into bubbly forms.

- **Standard Elements (Buttons, Cards, Modals)**: Standard radius of `0.5rem` (`rounded`), scaling to `1rem` (`rounded-lg`) on song preview cards and result panels.
- **Note Tiles**: Precision rounded rects (`0.5rem`) that preserve crisp structural edges for high-velocity readability.
- **Pill Exceptions**: Small HUD chips (e.g., multiplier tags, star badges, streak counters) use full pill shapes (`rounded-full`) to separate meta indicators from linear rhythm tiles.

## Components

### Rhythm Tiles (Falling Notes)
- **Standard Tap Tile**: High-refraction glass rectangle (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`), top border highlight (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`), inner vertical gradient shifting from Cyan (`#06B6D4`) to Violet (`#8B5CF6`).
- **Sustain/Hold Tile**: Translucent radiant beam with trailing glass tail, pulsates along an animated waveform spine.
- **Hit Reaction**: Instant expansion ring (`+12% scale`), opacity flash to pure white (`#FFFFFF`), then dissipates into chromatic particle embers.

### Floating Combo & Multiplier HUD
- Center-aligned display above the hit line.
- Dynamic typography: `display-score` in `Outfit` with numerical count-up animations.
- Multiplier pills: Space Grotesk `label-combo` styled with neon borders (`#F43F5E`) and magenta glow when active fever kicks in.

### Song Selection Cards
- Glass cards with blurred album art behind dark scrims (`rgba(11, 15, 25, 0.7)`).
- Clear star tier indicator (1 to 3 stars) rendered in high-chroma amber-gold with soft neon halo.
- High-score badge positioned in the top-right corner with Space Grotesk tabular data.

### Buttons & Action Triggers
- **Primary CTA ("Play", "Retry")**: Solid gradient fill from Primary (`#8B5CF6`) to Secondary (`#06B6D4`) with `rounded-lg`, bold text, and a permanent soft outer glow (`box-shadow: 0 4px 20px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary / Ghost CTA**: Translucent glass surface, 1px white border at 15% opacity, text in full white.

### Score & Progress Trackers
- Segmented linear song progress bar pinned to the top viewport edge: 2px height, filled with radiant cyan, marked with checkpoint crowns at 33%, 66%, and 100%.
